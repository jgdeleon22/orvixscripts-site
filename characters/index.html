FAQ

Questions about Orvix Characters

What does it replace?

Your multicharacter (esx_multicharacter, qb-multicharacter and similar), your identity or registration script, and qb-spawn on QBCore.

Will players lose their characters?

No. Existing ESX characters (char1, char2…) and QBCore characters show up as they are.

Which clothing scripts work?

illenium-appearance, fivem-appearance, qb-clothing, esx_skin / skinchanger and crm-appearance. It detects which one you run.

Can I change the scenes and spawns?

Yes. Stand where you want a spot, type /ocpos, and paste the line into the config.
