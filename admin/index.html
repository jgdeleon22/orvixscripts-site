FAQ

Questions about Orvix Admin

Does it replace txAdmin?

It sits next to it. txAdmin still starts and stops your server; Orvix Admin is where your staff actually work in game.

Are there player or staff limits?

No. One license covers one server with as many players and staff as you have.

How do bans stop alt accounts?

Bans save every identifier a player has: license, Discord, Steam, IP and hardware tokens. A new account on the same PC is still blocked.

Can staff abuse it?

Ranks decide what each person can do, lower ranks can never act on higher ones, every action is logged to Discord, and Staff watch flags suspicious staff behavior to owners.

Does it need a website or subscription?

No. Everything runs inside your server. Discord logs and screenshots are optional.

When is it out?

Orvix Admin launches after Pulse. The docs are up now so you can plan your setup.
