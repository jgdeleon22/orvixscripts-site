What's inside

Everything a citizen needs, in one pocket.

Tap an app to see what it does. Server owners can hide any of them, and players can add or remove them from their own home screen in the App Store.

Why Pulse

Built like a server owner would build it.

Nothing trusted from the client

Every text, payment, post and call is checked on the server. The UI can only reach a fixed list of actions, so modders can't fake messages or move money.

No SQL to import

Pulse creates and upgrades its own tables on first start. Copy the folder, add one ensure line, and you're live.

Your secrets stay secret

Photos and voice memos upload from the server, so your FiveManage token never reaches a player's game.

Works with what you run

Uses ESX bank accounts, pays esx_billing bills to the right society, and reads garage spots straight from cd_garage.

Real calls

Proper ringing, answering and hang-ups over pma-voice call channels, with a live call bar while you use other apps.

Easy to extend

Exports for texts, mail, notifications and bank history, so your jobs and shops can talk to players through the phone.

Setup

Running in four steps.

  1. Drop it inCopy pulse_phone into your resources folder.
  2. Ensure itAdd it to server.cfg after pma-voice, ox_lib and oxmysql.
  3. Add your tokenOne server-only line turns on the camera and voice memos.
  4. RestartTables are created automatically. Hand out a phone item and press F1.
server.cfg
# Pulse phone
ensure pulse_phone

# server-only: players never see this
set pulse_fivemanage_token "your-token"
