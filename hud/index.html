Player settings

Every player makes it their own.

Players type /hudsettings to change the HUD for themselves. It's saved on their PC and nobody else sees the change.

FAQ

Questions about Orvix Hud

Does it replace my other HUD scripts?

Usually, yes. It covers the status HUD, speedometer, seatbelt, cruise control and speed limits in one resource, so you can turn those scripts off.

Does it work with QBCore?

Yes. Orvix Hud works with ESX Legacy and QBCore and finds the one your server runs by itself. On QBCore it uses the same stress events as qb-hud, so your other scripts keep working.

Can I lock the look for everyone?

Yes. In the config you can stop players changing colours, text size or moving pieces, and set your own default colour and logo.

Can I set my own speed limits?

Yes. List any street with its limit in the config; every other street uses your default.
