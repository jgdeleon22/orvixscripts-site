Getting started

Pulse is one resource, pulse_phone, with 25 apps. It runs on ESX Legacy or QBCore and sets up its own database tables.

Install guideUp and running in five steps ConfigurationEvery option in config.lua TroubleshootingFixes for common problems

Installation

  1. Start your framework (es_extended or qb-core), oxmysql, ox_lib and pma-voice. Add screenshot-basic for the camera.
  2. Copy pulse_phone into resources and add ensure pulse_phone after the resources above.
  3. Turn off any other phone resource, then start the server.
TipThe full walkthrough with copy-ready lines is on the Install page.

Camera & voice memos

Photos, Blink snaps and voice memos are stored on FiveManage. Create a token in your FiveManage dashboard and add it to server.cfg:

server.cfg
set pulse_fivemanage_token "your-token"
Use setNever setr. That sends the token to every player's game.

Phone item & key

Players need a phone or iphone item by default. Change the names in Config.PhoneItems or set Config.RequireItem = false. The phone opens with F1; players can pick their own key in GTA Settings › Key Bindings › FiveM.

Ryde & GrubGo

Customers pay from their bank when they request a ride or place an order. The money is refunded automatically if they cancel, nobody accepts in time, or the worker disconnects.

  • Who can work: Config.Ryde.DriverJob and Config.GrubGo.CourierJob. false lets anyone go online, or set a job like 'taxi'.
  • Ryde fares: base fare plus a per-km price from pickup to the rider's map waypoint, with a minimum fare. ServiceCut is the share the app keeps.
  • GrubGo restaurants: each has a name, emoji, coordinates, menu and an optional society that receives the food money. Set GiveItems = true and an item per menu entry to hand out real food items.

{ name = 'Burger Shot', emoji = '🍔', coords = vector3(-1183.3, -884.2, 13.8), society = 'burgershot',

Cruise

Cruise sets the GTA map waypoint, so the normal minimap route shows the way. With drive mode on, an overlay shows the street, speed, route distance and ETA, and warns about reports ahead within Config.Cruise.AlertDistance.

Reports (police, crash, hazard, traffic, closed) are placed where the reporter is, confirmed by others, and removed after ReportMinutes or when drivers say they're gone. Add your city's landmarks to Config.Cruise.Places.

Dating & Blink

Spark is swipe-based and needs a profile photo. Kindred uses up to three prompts from Config.Dating.Prompts, likes with a note, and a daily like limit. Profiles are 18+ in character and never show phone numbers.

Blink snaps can be opened once and show for Config.Blink.ViewSeconds. Stories last 24 hours and are visible to the poster's contacts.

Services & 911

Each entry in Config.Services needs a job name that matches your job exactly. Dialing 911 or 912 opens a Police or EMS request; on-duty players get a dispatch list with GPS.

Updating

Copy the new files over the old ones, then run refresh and restart pulse_phone. Player data stays; new tables are added automatically.

Troubleshooting

Console says "framework: none"

Pulse started before your framework. Put ensure pulse_phone after es_extended or qb-core, or set Config.Framework to 'esx' or 'qb'.

The phone won't open with F1

Check the player has a phone item (or set Config.RequireItem = false), that they didn't rebind the key, and that no other phone resource is running.

Photos, snaps or voice memos don't upload

Make sure screenshot-basic is started and the token is set with set pulse_fivemanage_token. Restart after adding it and check the console for a FiveManage error.

Calls connect with no voice

Start pma-voice before Pulse and make sure no radio uses the channels from Config.CallChannelOffset (5000).

Nobody gets Ryde or GrubGo jobs

Drivers and couriers must tap "Go online" in the app. If you set a job in config, they also need that job.

Restaurant pickups say "not close enough"

Check the restaurant's coords in Config.GrubGo.Restaurants match your map, or raise PickupRange.

FAQ

Does Pulse work with QBCore?

Yes. Pulse supports ESX Legacy and QBCore and detects which one you run.

Do I need to import an SQL file?

No. Pulse creates and updates its own tables when it starts.

Can players remove apps they don't want?

Yes, from the App Store app. Their data is kept if they add the app back.

Can I add my own restaurants and places?

Yes. GrubGo restaurants and Cruise popular places are plain lists in config.lua.

Where do I buy Pulse?

Right here on orvixscripts.com, checkout through Tebex. It's in final testing now.
