Security

  • Every action is checked on the server. The phone screen can only reach a fixed list of actions.
  • Locations for Services, Ryde, GrubGo and Cruise reports are read on the server, never sent by the player.
  • Fares and orders are paid up front and refunded automatically if a job doesn't happen.
  • Dating apps, Chirp and Lens never show phone numbers.
  • Photos only come from the player's own gallery, and your FiveManage token never leaves the server.

Developer exports

Let your jobs, banks and shops talk to players through the phone.

server exports
exports.orvix_pulse:GetNumber(source)
exports.orvix_pulse:GetSourceByNumber('555-1234')
exports.orvix_pulse:HasPhone(source)
exports.orvix_pulse:IsInCall(source)
exports.orvix_pulse:SendMessage('Fleeca', '555-1234', 'Your loan was approved')
exports.orvix_pulse:Notify(source, { app = 'bank', title = 'Fleeca', text = 'Payment received' })
exports.orvix_pulse:LogTransaction(source, 2500, 'Paycheck')
exports.orvix_pulse:SendMail(source, { sender = 'LSPD', subject = 'Ticket issued', body = 'You were fined $150.' })
