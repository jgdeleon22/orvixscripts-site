Why Pulse

Built like a server owner would build it.

01

ESX or QBCore, no setup

Pulse detects your framework on start: jobs, money, items, vehicles, bills and admins all just work.

02

Nothing trusted from the client

Every text, payment, post and ride is checked on the server, and positions are read on the server, so they can't be faked.

03

Money that can't go missing

Ryde fares and GrubGo orders are paid up front and refunded if cancelled, expired or the worker disconnects.

04

No SQL to import

Pulse creates and upgrades its own tables. Copy the folder, add one line, start the server.

05

Your secrets stay secret

Photos and voice memos upload from the server, so your FiveManage token never reaches a player's game.

06

Easy to extend

Exports for texts, mail, notifications and bank history let your jobs and shops talk to players through the phone.
